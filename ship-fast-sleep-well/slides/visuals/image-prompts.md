Use picture zero as the style and character reference when you generate.

## assets/horror-friday.png (horror picture)

A hand-drawn ink sketch with light watercolor, 16:9, same style and same curly-haired developer with glasses as the reference. Friday, 16:00, a dark rainy window. The developer sits at a messy desk, frustrated, hands in hair, in front of a laptop with red error alerts. On the laptop an AI chat says "FIX IT NOW" (vibe coding). A pipeline progress bar crawls at 12 percent, labeled "PIPELINE: 45 MIN". A phone rings and buzzes with the label "PAGER! ON CALL". Handwritten uppercase labels around the scene: "NO RUNBOOK", "NO FEATURE FLAG", "LOGS WITHOUT CONTEXT", "TESTS NOBODY TRUSTS". Tangled cables, cold coffee, a wall clock showing 16:00. Dark red and grey watercolor accents, no photo realism.

## assets/target-state.png (target picture)

Same style and character. Friday, 16:00, sunny. The developer leans back relaxed and smiling, laptop closed, coffee in hand. Around them handwritten uppercase labels with small icons: "FAST PIPELINE" (progress bar at 100 percent, green check), "MEANINGFUL TESTS" (green checks in a safety net), "FEATURE FLAG: ON/OFF" (toggle), "STRUCTURED LOGS AND TRACES" (small dashboard), "ALERTS + RUNBOOK" (clipboard checklist), "CANARY TESTS" (small bird icon). The phone lies silent on the desk. Soft yellow and fresh green watercolor accents.

## Horror picture (slide 3): four concept variants

Shared style line, add to every prompt: "Hand-drawn ink sketch with light watercolor washes on off-white paper, 16:9, handwritten uppercase labels, same curly-haired developer with glasses as the reference image, dark red, orange and grey accents, no photo realism."

### A: Burning server room (chaos)

The developer stands in a server room. Two server racks burn with cartoon flames, cables hang like spaghetti, a smoke alarm blinks. A monitor shows "500 INTERNAL SERVER ERROR" and a red graph that falls down. A phone on the floor rings with "PAGER! ON CALL". Labels: "PROD IS DOWN", "NO RUNBOOK", "NO FEATURE FLAG". The developer holds a laptop with a chat window: "AI: FIX IT NOW (VIBE CODING)". A wall clock shows 16:00 and a calendar page says "FRIDAY".

### B: Split picture, mirror of picture zero (my favorite)

Same split-panel layout as picture zero, with the dashed divider in the middle. Left: sun and rocket from picture zero are replaced by a burning rocket crashing. The developer panics at the laptop, hair on end, red alerts around, a slow pipeline bar at 12 percent "PIPELINE: 45 MIN". Right: night, moon, the developer awake in bed at 3 AM, phone buzzing with "PAGER!", sweat drops, dark circles, a broken net labeled "NO SAFETY NET". This links directly to the target picture where the same panels are calm.

### C: The pager monster

A huge phone with angry eyes and teeth chases the small developer through an office at 16:00. Burning server icons in the background, flying error messages "NullPointerException", "TIMEOUT", "OOM". A tiny sign says "PIPELINE: 45 MIN". The developer runs with the laptop under the arm.

### D: Vibe coding gone wrong

The developer sits at a desk. From the laptop a robot hand pours code into the screen, and the code spills over the desk like spaghetti and sets the server on fire. The developer holds a "LGTM" sign with a nervous smile. Labels: "TESTS NOBODY TRUSTS", "WHAT DID THE AI CHANGE?", "PAGER!".
